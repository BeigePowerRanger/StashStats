# Product Guide: StashStats

## Initial Concept
Personal analytics tooling for Ravelry stashes, yarns, and projects.

## Target Audience
**Data-driven Crafter**: Focused on deep personal analytics, deriving insights from their knitting/crocheting data, and interacting with rich visualizations and statistics.

## Core Features
- **Project Velocity**: Analyzing the time spent on projects versus the amount of yarn used, allowing crafters to understand their crafting speed and habits.
- **Deep Search & Statistics**: Advanced filtering, querying, and computation of comprehensive statistics across yarns, stashes, and projects.

## Presentation & Interface
- **Web Dashboard**: An intuitive and visually appealing web interface providing dynamic graphs, charts, and data visualizations for the collected analytics.

## Long-term Vision
**Comprehensive API Client**: Ultimately evolving the project to provide full coverage of the Ravelry API, acting as a robust foundation for extensive personal analytics and future applications.
