## Architectural Decisions
- Standardized on synchronous execution (`RavelryClient` over `httpx.Client`) to eliminate method duplication and keep the codebase lean.
- Modularized schemas under `src/stashstats/models/` using pure attribute docstrings.
- Implemented `get_my_stash()` as a convenience wrapper around `get_stash_list()`.
- Enforced strict subagent delegation to `cavecrew` agents (`cavecrew-builder`, `cavecrew-investigator`, `cavecrew-reviewer`) using `flash` models whenever multi-file edits or bounded scope tasks arise to optimize token allocation limits.
