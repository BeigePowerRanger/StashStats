# Daily Diary - 2026-08-16

## Decisions
- **Separate Look & Feel from Implementation**: Focused entirely on visual hierarchy, user workflows, and domain calculations rather than Dash-specific callbacks or SQLite/Postgres specifics.
- **Accordion Grouping Pattern**: Preserved the parent-yarn grouped accordion design (Brand — Name) with nested colorway/pack rows to prevent visual repetition while maintaining exact pack-level tracking.
- **Local-First & Ledger Rollback**: Documented the dual-state model where user edits immediately reflect in the UI with a "Pending Sync" badge until pushed upstream, and ledger deletions automatically restore stash balances.
